My first game (Fliper Engine)

Neon Brawl: a small 2D pixel-art street brawler to start from. Walk the neon avenue, beat the gang, break barrels,
eat the chicken, take down the boss.
- Press F5 (or the Play button at the top right) to play it. Arrows/WASD move, Space punches (3 in a row = combo),
  Esc pauses, F11 fullscreen. A game controller works too (d-pad, left stick, A, Start).
- The FLIPER panel on the right is your AI: press "Sign in with Fliper" once, then tell it what to change
  ("add a second street", "make the hero a cat", "turn it into a 1-on-1 fighting game", "add a jump kick"...).
- All the art is in arte/ (PNG sprite sheets + a .json map of the animations per character).
- New games: Project > Quit to Project List, then "+ Create".
